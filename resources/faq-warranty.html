Making a claim

How a warranty claim gets resolved

01

Check the app first

Most fault codes show a plain-language description and a suggested fix directly in the Solvaris app before anything else is needed.

02

Contact your installer

They can read diagnostics remotely and resolve the majority of issues without a site visit.

03

Installer opens a claim

If the fault isn't resolved remotely, the installer opens a warranty claim with Solvaris support directly, using the unit's serial number.

04

Repair or replacement scheduled

Most claims are resolved with a part shipped to the installer within 5 working days; full unit replacement is scheduled directly with the homeowner if needed.
