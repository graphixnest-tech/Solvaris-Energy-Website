Solvaris StringLine

A string inverter for sites that don't need storage yet — built on the same firmware platform as the HX Series, so a battery can be added later without a second commissioning visit.

Why it's built this way

Designed around how inverters actually get used

  • Storage-ready from day one — Shares a communication protocol with the HX hybrid range, so upgrading to storage later is a hardware swap, not a redesign.
  • Scales into light commercial — The same product line runs from a small residential roof up to 50 kW, covering most warehouse and office installs.
  • Built-in export limiting — Meets local grid export caps out of the box, without an add-on relay or external controller.
