Product · EV charging

Solvaris FlowCharge

A home EV charger that watches your solar export in real time and shifts its current to match it — so the car charges on power you already made, not power bought back at retail rates.

Download datasheet Request a quote
22 kW  three-phase output
6–32 A  dynamic current range
IP54  outdoor rated

Why it's built this way

Designed around how ev chargers actually get used

  • Solar-matched charging — Reads inverter export every few seconds and ramps charging current up or down so grid draw stays near zero whenever there's surplus to use.
  • Scheduled and manual modes — Set off-peak windows, force a full charge before a trip, or leave it on Solar Only and let the system decide.
  • Shared with the household load — Talks to the hybrid inverter so EV charging never trips the main breaker when the oven and dryer are running too.
